## 5 Service Pillars (from Profile)

### 1. Giấy Phép Bay & Điều Phối (Flight Permits & Slot)
- **Scheduled Flight Setup**: FAOC, Traffic Right, AOSP
- **Permits**: Entry/Overflight permits, emergency handling
- **Medical permits**: Ambulance, Ferry flights
- **Slot coordination**: SGN, HAN, DAD
- **Monitoring**: Avoid cancellation penalties

### 2. Đại Diện Hãng & Quản Lý Khai Thác (Airline Representation & Station Management)
- **GSA**: Full representation, work with authorities
- **Office at airport**: Dedicated staff
- **Station Management**: Supervise check-in, boarding, cargo, ramp safety
- **Fuel & Catering coordination**

### 3. An Toàn & Điều Hành Bay (Safety & Flight Operations)
- **Safety audits**: IATA/ISAGO compliance
- **Flight Package**: NOTAM, Weather, Flight Plan
- **Load Control**: Weight & balance, Loadsheet
- **Crew support**: Flight planning assistance

### 4. VIP & Fast Track Services
- **Fast Track CIQ**: Priority immigration/customs
- **Private Jet**: Ground handling 5-star
- **Charter**: Charter flight consulting
- **VIP Lounge**: Premium lounge access
- **Meet & Greet**: Escort from aircraft door

### 5. Hậu Cần & Thương Mại (Logistics & Commercial)
- **Crew Concierge**: Visa (urgent), hotel 4-5*, transport
- **Corporate Travel**: ABTRIP ticketing
- **MICE**: Group tours
- **Cargo**: DGR, VAL, warehouse connection
