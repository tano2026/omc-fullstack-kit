## Tier Assignment Notes

- **Tier A**: Official government source (Federal Register, DOT), airline official, company official
- **Tier B**: Major industry reports (Statista, Grand View Research, IATA), published research, established industry analysis (Skift, AltexSoft)
- **Tier C**: Industry media (Travel Week, BTN), affiliate program data (Creator Hero, Lasso), community guides (Frommers, Going, Reddit consensus)
- Some numbers from aggregated sources (15-30% commission range from 5+ sources) are **Tier D** — logical inference from multiple Tier B/C sources, stated as range not precision
